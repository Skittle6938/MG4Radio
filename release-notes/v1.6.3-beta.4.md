**This update downloads about 8.6 MB of data: better on Wi-Fi from a phone hotspot, or over USB if possible.**

**Radio Dash 1.6.3 beta 4**

- **Favourite star easier to see**: on the station tiles of every band (DAB+, FM, AM), a station in your favourites now has a rounded square filled with the theme colour and a white star; the others a dark square with a light hollow star. The star button of the station playing, on the left, has the same look, a bit bigger.
- **Android Auto button icon** ([#5](https://github.com/Skittle6938/MG4Radio/issues/5)): with CarPlay or another app chosen in MG4Dash, the button of the bars now shows CarPlay's icon or that app's icon, not Android Auto's. Needs MG4Dash 1.7.3 beta 24 or later.

Install from MG4Dash (Settings > System > Update > Update Radio Dash, with beta versions on), or like before (`adb push` + `pm install -r -f`, or USB stick), then **reboot the head unit** (hold Home about 20 s): the steering wheel buttons don't reach the radio until you do.

Nécessite MG4Dash 1.7.3 bêta 24 ou plus récent.

Installation depuis MG4Dash (Réglages > Système > Mise à jour > Mettre à jour Radio Dash, avec les versions bêta activées), ou comme avant (`adb push` + `pm install -r -f`, ou clé USB), puis **redémarrez l'autoradio** (Accueil maintenu environ 20 s) : les boutons du volant n'atteignent la radio qu'après.
