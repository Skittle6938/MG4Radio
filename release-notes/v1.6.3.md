**This update downloads about 8.6 MB of data: better on Wi-Fi from a phone hotspot, or over USB if possible.**

**Radio Dash 1.6.3** — everything since 1.6.2 (betas 1 to 4). Install over 1.6.2 or any beta.

- **MG4Dash no longer switches back to DAB+ while you listen to FM** ([MG4Dash #80](https://github.com/Skittle6938/MG4Dash/issues/80)): the DAB+ tuner keeps receiving in the background during FM, and its info no longer reaches MG4Dash unless the radio is on DAB+.
- **DAB+ picture kept**: on a station that sends no logo, the player and MG4Dash keep its last slideshow picture.
- **RDS accents shown** (é, è, à, ç…) in the radio text and station names, in Radio Dash and in MG4Dash.
- **RDS station name in MG4Dash**: Radio Dash sends MG4Dash the name it receives with the frequency, so the music card shows the name instead of the frequency.
- **Left bar at full height** when MG4Dash's *Shortcuts menu* option hides MG's fog light button.
- **Favourite star easier to see**: on the station tiles of every band (DAB+, FM, AM), a rounded square filled with the theme colour and a white star for a favourite, a dark square with a light hollow star otherwise; the star button of the station playing has the same look, a bit bigger.
- **Android Auto button follows MG4Dash** ([#5](https://github.com/Skittle6938/MG4Radio/issues/5)): the button of the bars opens what MG4Dash's button opens (Android Auto, CarPlay or the app chosen with a long press on it in MG4Dash), with its name and icon. Needs **MG4Dash 1.7.3**; with an older MG4Dash it keeps opening Android Auto.

Install from MG4Dash (Settings > System > Update > Update Radio Dash), or like before (`adb push` + `pm install -r -f`, or USB stick), then **reboot the head unit** (hold Home about 20 s): the steering wheel buttons don't reach the radio until you do.

**Radio Dash 1.6.3** — tout depuis la 1.6.2 (bêtas 1 à 4). À installer par-dessus la 1.6.2 ou une bêta.
